# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in cryptofeed-rs to be safe, respectful, and technically
productive. Be considerate, assume good intent while evaluating evidence, and
criticize ideas and implementations rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, sustained
disruption, and publication of another person’s private information are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns confidentially to
[info@rsitech.ai](mailto:info@rsitech.ai). RSI Tech will review reports
privately, limit disclosure to what is necessary, and may edit or remove
content, reject contributions, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation.

Good-faith reports will not be retaliated against. Knowingly false or abusive
reports may themselves violate this policy.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing the project in
public or private project-related communication.
